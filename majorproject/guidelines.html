Community Guidelines

At Neighbors Helping Neighbors, we are committed to fostering a welcoming and supportive community for everyone. To ensure that our platform remains a safe and positive space for all members, we ask that you adhere to the following guidelines:

The TYPES of posts we allow:

There are 4 kinds of posts allowed on the site:

  • Requesting Help: If you are in need of assistance, please feel free to post a request. Be specific about the help you need and any relevant details.
  • Offering Help: If you have resources, skills, or time to offer, please share how you can assist others in the community. This could include volunteering, donating items, or providing services.
  • Sharing Resources: If you know of any local resources, events, or opportunities that could benefit the community, please share them here. This could include information about food banks, support groups, or community events.
  • Gratitude:We always like to hear about and share your stories of kindness and community! Please feel free to share these types of posts often!
