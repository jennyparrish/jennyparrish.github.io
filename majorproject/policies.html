Terms of Service

Neighbors Helping Neighbors is a space where people care about eachother, and the environment. This is a not-for-profit platform meant to help people who are in need; you won't find any capitalism here. Most folks want their items to go to a person who can use them and needs them, instead of to someone who is going to sell them at a markup. This is why we don't recommend the "first come, first served" model; we prefer the "let it simmer" approach, where the poster waits a day before choosing a recipient (and the choice is 100% up to the poster). Some people don't mind if resale is the receiver's motivation; but please, always respect when an offer says "no-resellers". Being reported for not respecting the giver's wishes may result in removal from the platform. Please be advised.

  1. Respect the privacy of others and do not share personal information without consent.
    Please see our Privacy Policy below
  2. Be kind and respectful to everyone.
  3. Provide accurate descriptions of the item(s) being offered (and pictures whenever possible).
  4. Do not post items that are illegal or prohibited by law.
  5. Do not engage in any form of harassment, discrimination, or hate speech.
  6. Follow all local laws and regulations when using the platform.
  7. Report any suspicious or inappropriate behavior to the platform administrators.
